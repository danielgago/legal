Cofrinho — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 June 2026 · Last updated: 3 June 2026

Cofrinho ("the app") is a personal finance app developed and operated by Daniel Gago, an individual developer based in Portugal ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and how. We do not sell your data, show advertising, or use third-party advertising or analytics tracking.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Daniel Gago, an individual developer based in Portugal. For any privacy question or request, contact danieljagago@gmail.com.

2. What data the app handles

  • Finance data you enter — transactions, categories, budgets and similar records you create in the app.
  • Account identity (optional) — if you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier and email address solely to authenticate you and to back up and sync your data across your devices. The app is usable offline without an account; signing in is required only for cloud backup and bank connections.
  • Bank account information (optional) — if you connect a bank, we access, with your explicit consent, your account details and transaction history for the accounts you authorise, through our regulated open-banking provider (see section 4).

3. How your data is stored and protected

  • Imported bank transactions start on your device. Transaction data retrieved from your bank is initially stored only locally on your phone, in the app's review inbox. If you choose to save or import these into the app as regular transactions, they become part of your finance records and may then be included in optional cloud backup and sync, on the same encrypted basis as any other finance record you create (see below).
  • Synced finance data is encrypted. The finance records you choose to back up are encrypted on your device with a per-user key before being stored by our cloud backend, so the stored payload is not readable as plain text by the backend.
  • Cloud backup and authentication are provided by Supabase, acting as our processor.

4. Open banking provider

Bank connections are provided by Enable Banking, a licensed third-party account-information service provider (AISP) operating under PSD2. When you connect a bank, you authenticate directly with your bank and grant consent through your bank's own secure authentication. We never see or store your bank login credentials. Consent is granted for a limited period (up to ~90 days, set by your bank) and you can revoke it at any time by disconnecting in the app or through your bank. Enable Banking's own privacy terms apply to their role as provider: enablebanking.com.

5. Legal basis (GDPR)

We process your data on the basis of your consent (for connecting a bank and for optional cloud sync) and to perform the service you request (running the app). Where processing is necessary to provide the services you have requested, it may also be based on the performance of a contract with you. You may withdraw consent at any time.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your data and do not share it for advertising. Data is shared only with the processors needed to run the service you enable — our cloud backend (Supabase) for backup/authentication and our open-banking provider (Enable Banking) for bank connections. We have entered into appropriate data processing agreements with our processors where required by law.

7. International data transfers

Some of our processors may store or process data on servers located outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Where data is transferred outside the EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards as required by the GDPR — such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an applicable adequacy decision — so that your data continues to receive an equivalent level of protection.

8. Retention and deletion

Local data lives on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. You can clear all local and backed-up data from within the app at any time ("Delete all data"). To request deletion of any remaining backed-up data, contact us at the email above. Following an account deletion or deletion request, any remaining backed-up data is removed from our active cloud backend within 30 days; residual copies in routine encrypted backups are overwritten on the normal backup rotation, within 90 days.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, and port your data, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact danieljagago@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Portugal, the CNPD).

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change accordingly. Material changes will be reflected in the app.
